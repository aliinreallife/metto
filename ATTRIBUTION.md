
## Isfahan review preview

`data/reference/isfahan-candidate-preview.json` is a review-only projection of
metto-data commit `1e83f67df3b3dd8e599eb416c42ed93a2a2bee7f`, derived from
OpenStreetMap data © OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL-1.0:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . It is not a released passenger network.

The original review projection is retained as historical evidence. A separate
owner-authorized release now enables the bounded Isfahan Line 1 network and
timetable. Metto-curated collection credits link to
https://github.com/aliinreallife/metto-data, which records the original source
URLs and hashes. Rail geometry remains derived from OpenStreetMap and retains
the copyright and ODbL attribution above.
